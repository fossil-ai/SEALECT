Maritime scene realism — a quick visual study

Thanks for helping. This takes about 15 minutes and is fully anonymous.

What you'll do

You'll see pairs of harbour/sea photos. The two pictures in a pair show the same scene, but each has a different boat added to it — the added boat is outlined with a red box. For each pair, pick the picture whose outlined boat looks more realistic for that scene — a believable orientation, size and position on the water.

  • Only judge the red-boxed boat; the other boats are just part of the original scene.
  • Judge the geometry (heading, scale, placement), not photo quality, colour or sharpness.
  • Go with your first instinct — there are no right answers. Use “Can’t tell” only if truly tied.
  • Your progress is saved in this browser, so you can stop and come back.
